The core workflow

Less time entering. More time caring.

The initial product is focused on the repeatable moments that slow down emergency department documentation.

01

Speak naturally

Planned: create clinical notes with streaming speech recognition, automatic punctuation, and medical terminology. Typed charting works in this build.

02

Stay in the workflow

Navigate sections, insert templates, and correct text in a focused charting workspace. Voice commands are planned; editing is typed today.

03

Share securely

Review and export charts through controlled PDF, Word, text, and mobile sharing workflows, with an audit trail of every export.

Product roadmap

Start focused. Expand responsibly.

The first release centers on secure charting and export. The list below is what the build is working toward — not everything on it is shipped yet. Advanced device pairing and EHR integrations follow after the core workflow is reliable.

  • →ER-specific chart templates
  • →Reusable AutoText and snippets
  • →Desktop and mobile browser support
  • →Account access and role-based security
  • →Auditable chart exports
  • →Smartphone microphone pairing
